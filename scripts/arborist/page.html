A chain of Stumps · optional

    
A chain links several Stumps over Wi-Fi, so their #wifi-mesh chat, their shared rooms and their files go between them.
      With one Stump, leave it on “Not in a chain”: nothing else is needed, and the Stump works fully on its own.
      You can make it part of a chain later, from the Settings tab, without losing its name, its chat or its files.

    
    
    
    
    

      
      
      
With the home Wi-Fi above and a chain secret, the others can also join it over the router.

    About this page

    Everything is in this one file: nothing is downloaded or sent anywhere. It holds the Stump @@RELEASE@@: its images for the ESP32-S3-CAM and the ESP32-WROVER (MicroPython @@MICROPYTHON@@, MIT licence, micropython.org, with the Stump built in) when the release has them, the Stump's files, MicroPython for the ESP32-S3 with octal PSRAM, and esptool-js (Apache-2.0, Espressif), which writes them. Each file's SHA-256 is checked on the board after it's copied. Settings are written by the Stump's own settings.py, as from /admin.
    

    The Heltec's Wi-Fi is set up by sending the RNode firmware its documented serial commands (KISS frames). This page contains none of the RNode firmware (GPLv3, Mark Qvist / unsigned.io) nor of rnodeconf (Reticulum License): the Heltec must already run that firmware, flashed once with the Provisioner.
